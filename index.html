Eight steps. No shortcuts.

“The point of being a detailer is actually hitting the spots maybe no one will ever see.”

Jasperson
  1. 01

    Wheels, barrels & tires first

    Brake dust gets broken down and brushed out of the barrel and the back of the spokes, not just wiped off the face.

    Cool Guy by Shine Supply
  2. 02

    Wells, frame & suspension

    On a lifted truck, everything behind the wheel is on display. Liners, coilovers and frame rails get degreased like they're paint.

    Gyeon APC
  3. 03

    Snow-foam prewash

    Thick foam sits and lifts the grit before a mitt ever touches the paint. Less dirt dragged around means fewer swirls.

    KCx Gs
  4. 04

    Hand contact wash

    Panel by panel with clean microfiber and a slick wash so the mitt glides instead of grabbing.

    Shift by Shine Supply
  5. 05

    Spot-free rinse

    The rinse comes from the van's own deionized tank instead of the hose, so it dries clean in full sun.

    Deionized water
  6. 06

    Blow-dry, then towel

    Air pushes water out of mirrors, badges, grilles and lug nuts so nothing drips later. Plush towels finish the flat panels.

    Metrovac Air Force + The Rag Company
  7. 07

    Glass & ceramic topper

    Streak-free glass and a ceramic spray sealant on the paint for gloss that sheds the next dust storm.

    Tech's Choice ceramic spray
  8. 08

    Tires dressed right

    A rich, even finish that doesn't sling down the side of your truck on the first drive.

    Decked Out by Shine Supply

The story

Started with a pressure washer.

A little over three years ago it was a pressure-washing business: driveways, concrete, commercial jobs. A month or two in, a Phoenix landscaping company asked him to keep their fleet clean. That's where detailing clicked.

He quit his full-time job to go all in. The van got built out, the process got dialed, and the trucks started showing up on his page every day.

When the page hit 2,000 followers, he didn't run a discount. He gave away a 2008 Tahoe, plus two free full details.

Before you book.

Do I need to provide water or power?

No. The van carries its own deionized water and generator. Just leave room to park next to the vehicle.

How does a recurring plan work?

You pick weekly, every 2 weeks or monthly, and you get the same day and arrival window every time. Every visit is the full Signature Wash process.

Can you work at my office or apartment?

Usually, yes. Some complexes don't allow washing on site, so check with them first. Interiors are almost always fine.

How long does a full detail take?

Plan on 4 to 6 hours for most vehicles, longer for lifted trucks. Ceramic coating is a 1 to 2 day job.

Is a ceramic coating worth it in Phoenix?

For a vehicle that lives outside in this sun, it's the best protection you can buy. It lasts when it's maintained, which is what the plans are for.

Do you do RVs, boats or fleets?

Yes. They're on the side of the van for a reason. Text a photo and the size for a quote.

TDS 000 ppm

Don't use Arizona water.

Phoenix tap water is loaded with dissolved minerals. Let it dry on dark paint in July and it leaves spots that etch. Jasperson rinses with deionized water that reads zero on the meter, then lets it sit on a black Escalade in 102° sun to prove it.

Result: no water spots, even in direct sunlight.

The story

He quit his full-time job to go all in. The van got built, the process got dialed, and the trucks started showing up on his page every day.

Before you book

Do I need to provide water or power?

No. The van carries its own deionized water and generator. Just make sure there's room to park next to the vehicle.

Can you work at my office or apartment?

Usually, yes. Some complexes don't allow washing on site, so check with them first. Interiors are almost always fine.

How long does a full detail take?

Plan on 4 to 6 hours for most vehicles. Lifted trucks and The Rig package run longer. Ceramic coating is a 1 to 2 day job.

Is a ceramic coating worth it in Phoenix?

For a vehicle that lives outside in this sun, it's the best protection you can buy. It lasts when it's maintained, which is why plans exist.

What if it's monsoon season?

If weather moves in, you get moved to the next open slot.

Do you do RVs, boats or fleets?

Yes. They're on the side of the van for a reason. Text a photo and the size for a quote.

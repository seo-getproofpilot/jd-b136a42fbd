      Before you book.

      Do I need to provide water or power?

      No. The van carries its own deionized water and generator. Just leave room to park next to the vehicle.

      How do maintenance plans work?

      Pick weekly, every 2 weeks or monthly. You're billed monthly, you get the same day and arrival window every visit, and every visit is the full Signature Wash. Cancel anytime.

      Which package should I pick?

      Signature Wash keeps a clean vehicle clean. Interior Deep Clean is for the inside only. Full Detail does both, top to bottom. Ceramic Coating is long-term protection for the paint.

      How long does it take?

      A Signature Wash is about 1.5 to 2 hours, a Full Detail 4 to 6 hours, and ceramic coating 1 to 2 days. Lifted trucks, RVs and boats run longer.

      Can you work at my office or apartment?

      Usually, yes. Some complexes don't allow washing on site, so check with them first. Interiors are almost always fine.

      Do you do fleets, RVs, boats or bikes?

      Yes. RV and boat pricing is on the van doors above. For fleets and bikes, text a photo to 602-616-0058 and Logan will quote it.
